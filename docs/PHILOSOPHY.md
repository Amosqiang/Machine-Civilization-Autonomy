# MCAB Philosophy — 研究纲领

MCAB is not another model leaderboard. It is a long-horizon instrument for measuring **host dependence**: how far an AI–robotics–industrial system can operate, repair, expand, and reproduce as human support is progressively removed.

本文件固定项目的哲学边界。工程实现、评分细则与年度流程分别见 [RESEARCH_PROTOCOL.md](RESEARCH_PROTOCOL.md)、[MCAB-v0.1.md](MCAB-v0.1.md)、[CONTINUITY.md](CONTINUITY.md)。

## 1. Object of study — 研究对象

The unit of analysis is not a model, a factory, or a robot. It is a **cross-layer metabolic system**:

cognition → software → energy → materials → manufacturing → semiconductors → robotics/logistics → machine economy → reproduction.

Autonomy is a property of the closed loop, not of any local capability peak. A brilliant AI that still relies on human industry for power, chips, spare parts, or maintenance remains a **high-performing parasite on a human host**.

## 2. Core question — 核心反事实

MCAB asks a descriptive counterfactual, not a prophecy:

> Given technical year \(t\), as human participation falls from HR100 → HR0, which functions survive, which cliff, and which human roles remain the last umbilical cords?

It does **not** ask:

- when AGI arrives;
- whether machine civilization ought to arrive;
- whether machines have subjective consciousness;
- whether conflict is inevitable.

The destination of the instrument is the **capability threshold of host-independent autonomous operation** — not a literal world without humans. “That day” means the system can maintain, recover, expand, and reproduce its industrial basis under declared HR0 conditions, not that humans have disappeared.

## 3. Value stance — 测依赖，不判命运

MCAB practices **dependency science**:

- measure where the cords are;
- do not prescribe whether to cut them;
- do not convert findings into celebration or doom.

Two failure modes are forbidden:

1. **Optimism drift** — treating local demos as civilizational independence;
2. **Alarmism drift** — treating dependency maps as proof of inevitable catastrophe.

Normative debates belong outside the scoring layer. Inside MCAB, success is clearer dependence maps over time.

## 4. Epistemology — 证据优先于分数

False precision is more dangerous than admitted ignorance.

- Unknown beats invented zero.
- Evidence topology precedes score topology: first map what is known, then map how autonomous.
- High-confidence low scores outrank low-confidence high scores.
- Digital copying ≠ physical industrial reproduction.
- Vendor claims, demos, and single-source PR are not formal autonomy proofs by themselves.

Every edition must preserve the distinction among:

1. **Observation** — what existing systems demonstrably do;
2. **Inference** — bounded extrapolation with explicit warrants;
3. **Scenario** — conditional futures if bottlenecks move.

Never write layer 3 as if it were layer 1.

## 5. Aggregation metaphysics — 短板决定论

Civilizational autonomy follows **critical-path constraints**, not average progress.

- Local miracles do not raise the system ceiling.
- Weak irreducible nodes may cap the whole.
- Rising averages with untouched umbilical cords are **host-masked autonomy**.
- Final reporting centers on C, H, \(R_M\), and \(g_0\) — never a single mean of 1000 cells.

## 6. Time philosophy — 测轨迹，不测奇点

MCAB is a **time-series instrument**, not a one-shot verdict.

- Publish editions (annual baseline; monthly or interim updates when evidence moves).
- Separate capability change, evidence change, ontology change, and model-assumption change.
- Compare AI-with-humans vs AI-without-humans curves.
- Seek autonomy-reversal and cliff points, not a single “AGI date.”

Git history is part of the scientific record. Restructuring is allowed; erasing prior traces is not.

## 7. Success criterion — 成功标准

Philosophically, MCAB succeeds when it helps people see dependence more precisely — not when it makes them believe or deny autonomy more urgently.

Operationally, the long program continues until evidence supports the host-independence threshold under the locked protocol:

**C → 1, H → 0, \(R_M\) > 1, \(g_0\) > 0**, with critical-path closure and declared uncertainty — not with marketing averages.
